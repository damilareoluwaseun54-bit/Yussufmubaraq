My Research Journey

I am a biomedical researcher with an MSc in Drug Research and Development from the University of Lagos, Nigeria. My academic interests centre on understanding disease mechanisms and investigating therapeutic approaches that can contribute to better health outcomes.


My master's research examined the effects of plant-derived therapeutics on oxidative stress biomarkers and histopathological alterations in a model of type 2 diabetes. This experience strengthened my interest in metabolic disease, experimental research and translational medicine.


I am interested in pursuing further research training and collaborating with scientists working to understand chronic diseases at the molecular and cellular levels.

Research Vision

From Laboratory Research to Public Health Impact

My long-term vision is to contribute to research and education that improve our understanding of diabetes and other chronic diseases, particularly in settings where research capacity and access to preventive healthcare need further development.


I hope to build collaborations across molecular biology, immunology, therapeutics and public health, with the broader goal of translating scientific discoveries into meaningful health benefits.
