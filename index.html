01 / About

From laboratory findings to human health

I am an emerging translational medicine researcher bridging bench-level biochemistry and clinical application. My MSc thesis investigated oxidative stress biomarkers and histopathological outcomes in a diet-chemical-induced Type 2 diabetes rat model. My research background spans molecular toxicology, medicinal-plant pharmacology and nanomedicine drug delivery, complemented by practical experience in clinical diagnostics and blood-transfusion laboratory procedures.

I aim to extend this bench-to-bedside trajectory through doctoral research focused on metabolic disease, biomarker discovery and innovative therapeutic strategies for chronic disease management.

03 / Education

Academic background

M.Sc. Drug Research and Development

University of Lagos, Nigeria · Nov 2024 – Dec 2025

Hybrid programme with online coursework and on-campus laboratory research and thesis.

Thesis: “Effect of RAGV (1:1) extract on oxidative stress biomarkers and histopathological alterations in a Fructose–STZ induced Type 2 Diabetes rat model.”

Investigated antioxidant and tissue-protective effects of a combined plant-extract regimen in an induced-diabetes rodent model, generating biomarker and histopathology data relevant to translational diabetes therapeutics.

B.Sc. (Hons) Biochemistry

Lagos State University, Nigeria · Nov 2016 – Sep 2021

Thesis: “Hypoglycemic and Antioxidant Activities of Carica papaya Leaf Extracts.”

Evaluated the hypoglycemic and antioxidant potential of C. papaya leaf extracts. This research trajectory connects my undergraduate work on medicinal plants with my later investigation of oxidative stress in experimental diabetes.

Relevant coursework: Experimental Biochemistry, Advanced Enzymology, Membrane Biochemistry, Microbial Biochemistry, Plant Biochemistry, Protein Chemistry, Clinical Biochemistry, Forensic Biochemistry, Genetic Engineering, Immunology and Biochemical Pharmacology.
